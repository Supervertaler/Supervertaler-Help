Supervertaler gives many of its features a name of their own; this page says in one line what each one is, and which products have it.

The four products are **Supervertaler for Trados**, **Supervertaler for memoQ**, **Supervertaler Sidekick** and **Supervertaler Workbench**. In the table, each product name links to that product's page about the feature.

## Easily confused

**SuperMemory or SuperVault?** SuperMemory holds *notes* that you write for the AI: what a client wants and why. SuperVault holds *translations*: it is your own lifetime translation memory, filled from your TMs, which Trados searches like any other TM. One is read by the AI as instructions; the other gives you matches.

**SuperSearch or SuperLookup?** SuperSearch is in the Trados plugin and searches the files of the project you have open, so you can find and replace across them. SuperLookup is in Workbench and looks a word up in your termbases, translation memories and web resources.

**TermLens or TermPicker?** Both show the same termbase matches for the current segment. TermLens shows them in the sentence, under the words they translate. TermPicker shows them as a numbered list, for quick insertion.

**QuickLauncher or QuickTrans?** QuickLauncher runs one of *your* AI prompts on the current segment. QuickTrans translates the selected text with several engines and lets you pick a result.
